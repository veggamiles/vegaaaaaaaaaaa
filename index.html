Key Features to Make Your Legislative Management System Effortless

Ordinance and Resolution Tracking

Comprehensive tracking system for all legislative documents with automated workflows and status updates.

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  1. Create new ordinance/resolution entry with metadata
  2. Assign tracking number and classification
  3. Set workflow stages and approval requirements
  4. Track progress through legislative process
  5. Generate status reports and notifications
  6. Archive completed items with full audit trail

Session and Meeting Management

Complete meeting lifecycle management from scheduling to minute generation with automated publishing.

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  1. Schedule legislative sessions and meetings
  2. Create and distribute meeting notices
  3. Manage attendee lists and quorum tracking
  4. Record meeting proceedings and votes
  5. Generate and approve meeting minutes
  6. Publish approved minutes to public portal

Legislative Agenda and Calendar

Integrated calendar system with automated agenda creation and collaborative planning tools.

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  1. Access integrated legislative calendar
  2. Add agenda items with supporting documents
  3. Collaborate on agenda development
  4. Set agenda approval workflows
  5. Publish finalized agendas
  6. Track agenda item outcomes and follow-ups

Committee Management System

Streamlined committee operations with member management, scheduling, and document distribution.

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  1. Set up committee structures and memberships
  2. Manage committee member roles and permissions
  3. Schedule committee meetings and hearings
  4. Distribute committee materials and documents
  5. Track committee recommendations and reports
  6. Coordinate inter-committee communications

Voting and Decision-Making System

Electronic voting platform with real-time results and comprehensive voting history tracking.

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  6. Maintain comprehensive voting history database
